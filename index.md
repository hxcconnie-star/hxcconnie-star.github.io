## About Me

I am a Research Assistant in the Department of Family Medicine and Primary Care, The University of Hong Kong, where I work on pharmacoepidemiology of non-communicable diseases using large-scale cohort data. Previously, I was based in the School of Public Health, working on psychiatric epidemiology with a focus on youth mental health. 

Before entering academic research, I worked as a Medical Data Analyst at Guangzhou AID Cloud Technology, a health-tech company applying big data and AI to healthcare, where I contributed to feature development and analysis of health and medical records to support both clinical and research applications.

I hold a dual Bachelor's degree in Applied Mathematics and Economics & Statistics, and a Master's degree in Health Informatics from Weill Cornell Medical College. I am currently seeking PhD opportunities in Biomedical Informatics/Epidemiology/Biostatistics.


## Research Interests

- **Predictive Modeling:** developing AI/ML-based models for disease risk prediction and clinical outcome forecasting, with attention to model generalizability and clinical utility
- **Pharmacoepidemiology:** studying medication utilization, safety, and effectiveness for non-communicable diseases (e.g., hypertension, diabetes) on large-scale population cohorts
- **Psychiatric Epidemiology:** investigating mental health outcomes and risk factors, with prior work focused on youth populations
- **Real-World Evidence (RWE):** using real-world data, such as electronic health records and population-based cohorts, to generate evidence that informs clinical and health policy decision-making
